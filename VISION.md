# STAR Framework Vision

## Mission

STAR is an open-source framework for downscaling coarse-resolution thermal remote sensing products using higher-resolution optical observations.

The framework aims to provide a simple, modular and scientifically rigorous environment for developing, validating and operationally applying thermal downscaling methods.

## Objectives

- Reproduce published STAR methods.
- Facilitate the development of new STAR-based algorithms.
- Support multiple combinations of thermal and optical sensors.
- Automate data downloading, preprocessing, calibration and operational processing.
- Produce reproducible scientific workflows.

## Design Principles

1. Simplicity before complexity.
2. Scientific rigor before optimization.
3. One responsibility per module.
4. Reusable components.
5. Open-source and reproducible.
6. Sensor-independent architecture whenever possible.
7. Every new paper should become a new capability of STAR rather than a separate codebase.
